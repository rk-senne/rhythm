## Description
> **Rhythm structures your day into focus cycles aligned with your body's natural
> ultradian rhythm — the 60–90 minute wave of energy that neuroscience says drives
> deep work.** Then it wraps each cycle in a short *transition ritual*: a guided
> breath, a sip of water, and a one-line reflection. Work deeply, rest
> intentionally.
>
> Unlike rigid 25-minute timers or 20-minute meditations, Rhythm fits how you
> actually work.
>
> • **Focus timer** — 45/60/90-minute cycles with a Live Activity on your Lock Screen
> • **Transition ritual** — guided breathing, hydration, and a micro-journal
> • **Gentle streaks** — rest days count; we never punish a missed day
> • **Apple Watch & widgets** — your rhythm, always a glance away
> • **Hydration in Apple Health** — logged automatically during rituals
> • **Weekly AI insight** (Pro) — a warm summary of your week
> • **Private by design** — your reflections are yours
>
> Free: 3 focus cycles a day. Pro unlocks unlimited cycles, custom durations,
> AI insights, and journal export.
>
> Calm productivity for people who've outgrown rigid timers but aren't ready to
> become monks.

- **User Content** (journal entries): stored for the user; not shared/sold.

  you add an analytics SDK (see `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` §7) — declare it if you do.
