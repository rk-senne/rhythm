**Document Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 29 May 2026
**Status:** Draft

---

## 1. Problem Statement

Knowledge workers face two opposing forces:

1. **Productivity pressure** — Deep focus, output metrics, packed calendars
2. **Wellness awareness** — Burnout, dehydration, sedentary habits, mental fatigue

Current solutions force users to choose: productivity apps ignore wellness, wellness apps ignore work context. No product treats the **transition between work blocks** as a first-class experience.

---

## 2. Target Market

### Primary Persona: "The Intentional Professional"

- Age 25–40, knowledge worker (developer, designer, writer, analyst)
- Uses iPhone + Apple Watch
- Already tried Pomodoro/focus apps but found them too rigid or too simple
- Cares about health but doesn't have time for 20-min meditation sessions
- Has 4-8 meetings/week and needs to protect deep work time

### Secondary Persona: "The ADHD Optimizer"

- Needs external structure to manage time
- Benefits from gentle nudges and rituals
- Values the journaling as a "what did I just do?" anchor
- High willingness to pay for tools that work

### Tertiary Persona: "The Wellness-Curious Manager"

- Wants their team to have sustainable work habits
- Would champion a Team tier purchase
- Cares about team burnout metrics

## 5. Value Proposition

**For the user:** "One app replaces your focus timer, water reminder, breathwork app, and micro-journal — and it knows your calendar."

**For teams:** "Shared focus hours reduce interruptions. Transition rituals reduce burnout. Managers get visibility without surveillance."

**Core insight:** The 2-hour ultradian rhythm is backed by neuroscience (Peretz Lavie, Nathaniel Kleitman). Humans naturally cycle between high focus and recovery every 90-120 minutes. Rhythm doesn't impose an arbitrary schedule — it aligns with your biology.

## 6. Business Model

| Tier | Price | Target |
|------|-------|--------|
| Free | $0 | Acquisition — 2 cycles/day, basic timer + hydration |
| Pro | $4.99/mo · $39.99/yr | Individual power users — unlimited cycles, Watch, calendar, AI |
| Team | $7.99/user/mo | Teams of 5+ — shared focus, Slack sync, analytics |

**Why subscription:** Ongoing value (AI summaries, calendar sync, cloud storage for journals). One-time purchase doesn't fund server costs or continued development.

**Why freemium over trial:** Wellness habits take 2-3 weeks to form. A 7-day trial doesn't give users enough time to feel the value. Free tier with limited cycles lets them build the habit, then upgrade when they want more.

## 8. Risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Notification fatigue → uninstall | High | High | Bundle all prompts into ONE per cycle |
| "Just another timer app" perception | Medium | High | Brand around the ritual, not the timer |
| Low retention after novelty wears off | High | High | Streak mechanics, AI insights, data lock-in |
| Apple restricts Focus mode API access | Low | Medium | Graceful fallback to manual toggle |
| Competitor (Apple/Google) builds native version | Low | High | Move fast, build data moat, community |
